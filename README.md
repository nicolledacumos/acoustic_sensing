# acoustic_sensing

This lab focuses on acoustic sensing and identifying different sounds. For the purpose of this lab, I aimed to identify claps, voices, and knocks. I also used the identification to perform basic tasks on Spotify, such as skip, previous song, and play/pause. 

To identify the sounds, I performed various gestures and noted the frequency of the sounds. I used the microphone fft bins and microphone spectrogram to get a visual and values. From here, I used AI to help create a way to identify these sounds through code. When a sound is identified, it is shown in the terminal of VS Code. 

With the sounds identified, I wanted to make my interactive system relate to something I use in my day-to-day life. I took inspiration from a classmate of mine who used capacitive touch sensing to control Spotify and created a system that controls Spotify through sound. 

The controls are as follows:
- Knock: Skip
- 2 claps: previous song
- 1 clap: play/pause

Because some sounds in songs can trigger the controls, it is best to wear headphones when doing this lab. I am still trying to troubleshoot how to have these sounds be detected with other noises, such as tv background noise, animals playing, etc.
